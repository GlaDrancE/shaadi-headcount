# Debate transcript — 11 Sep 2026

Three agents: Proponent and Skeptic researched independently (round 1), each rebutted the other (round 2), a Judge ruled on evidence. Full arguments condensed; URLs as cited by the agents.

- India is #1 country for Nano Banana usage; Gemini #1 on Indian app stores Sept 2025 (TechCrunch). Demand for the artefact is national-scale.
- People pay for done-for-you free-AI photos: Remini $3.73M in one week at $9.99/wk while free tools existed; Gemini iOS revenue $115K→$1.6M/mo Jan→Aug 2025.
- Boutique sellers already pay: CatalogX ₹299/20, Pro ₹849/mo; VERA ₹399/10; Botika ~₹2,000+; studio ₹15K+.

- Unit economics: B1 25 gens ≈ ₹85, B2 15 Pro gens ≈ ₹170.
- Funnel: week-1 expected ≈ ₹1,600. Odds: stranger pays 60% · five customers 40% · ₹10K/7d 12% · /14d 35%.
- Weakest point admitted: no Indian IG seller found charging ₹199–499 for AI photoshoots; no one complaining free Gemini isn't good enough.

## Round 1 — Skeptic

- Socialinsider Feb-2026 (35M posts): 1–5K-follower accounts average 580 Reel views; Ayush is below that tier.
- ₹200 of ads ≈ 10–25 landing visits; ad set never leaves learning; ~0 payments expected.

- Try-on models struggle with stripes/patterns/text (arxiv 2411.10499); sarees/jewellery are all pattern.
- Trust inverted: persona fronts, UPI to a different real name, no escrow, GitHub Pages URL.
- Odds: 35% · 20% · 4% · 10%. Concedes: distribution decoupled from followers; one 50K Reel flips everything; downside ~10h.

## Ruling — Judge
**Verdict:** Skeptic wins on economics and reach; Proponent wins on category existence and on converting objections into plan changes. ₹10K in 7 days is not realistic for this funnel. Run anyway as a ≤₹300, ≤10-hour test of one disputed assumption, with hard kill gates; treat any paid stranger as the win.

**Decisive findings:** B1 is a Flash product or dead at ₹299. Reels carry no tappable link — Reel → profile → bio → page at 0.5–1%, so two Reels ≈ 6–12 visits, not 60; every Proponent downstream number inherits this. Both sides' India CPM/CPC figures were unsourced — discounted.

**Reconciled odds:** one stranger pays 40% · five customers 15% · ₹10K in 7 days 3% · ₹10K in 14 days 10%.

**Agreed plan with gates:**
- Day 1 (≤₹100, 2h): paid billing; caps B1 12 Flash / B2 15 Pro; one redo. Generate 3 real B2 outputs (kurti + jewellery) on Pro, same prompts on Flash and free app. GATE: blind 5-follower poll — Pro must win ≥4/5 or B2 is dead; run B1 only.
- Day 2 (2h): above-the-fold = Ayush's real name, matching UPI payee, "3 watermarked shots free, pay to unlock"; ₹99 3-shot rung; same name on Reel end-cards.
- Days 3–4 (2h): B2 as Trial Reel (side-by-side vs free-tool failure); B1 Reel to followers. ₹0 ads. GATE end of day 4: <10 landing visits → stop producing; ≥1 sample request → proceed.
- Days 5–6 (2h): fulfil samples within 12h. GATE: ≥1 payment → ≤₹200 boost on that Reel; 0 payments after ≥3 samples → stop.
- Day 7 (1h): tally; write the build log either way.

**Conditions to run:** cash exposure ≤₹300, API first, ads only after a payment; time ≤10h including fulfilment; Day-1 blind test binding; B1 Flash-only and demoed as such; no charge before a watermarked sample; real name + matching payee above the fold and on every end-card; success = one paid stranger.

**Not covered by either side:** fulfilment labour (45–60 min/order); consent and minors among young followers sending selfies, plus Google's real-people policy and SynthID; persona disclosure and refunds — one "scam" screenshot ends the account. Put the disclosure in the bio.
