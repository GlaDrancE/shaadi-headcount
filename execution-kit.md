# Execution kit, week of 11 Sept 2026

Path chosen: **Fiverr + Instagram organic, ₹0 spent.** Ads only after a stranger pays.

Why this and not the brief as written (short version, the long version is in the chat):

- The Gemini image API has no free tier (Google's pricing page). Demo sets and orders are made in the **Gemini app** (20 free images a day) until a customer pays.
- ₹200 of ads buys 4 to 30 clicks in India, not 40 to 120. It cannot produce a signal, so no ads until there is a payment to scale.
- A boutique owner can make one shot free in five minutes. So the offer is now **a consistent catalogue** (same model, same light, checked, redone) with a **free preview before payment**. That kills the trust hop the brief admitted it could not solve.
- Your Fiverr history (15+ orders, 5.0, 100% on time) is the only channel with existing trust and inbound buyers. It costs ₹0 and about 2 hours to relist.
- Time, not money, is the binding constraint (job, B.Tech, interviews). Everything below fits in about 8 hours. Nothing is built until someone pays.

Log in and note: seller level, whether old gigs still exist (paused or active), whether the 15+ reviews are visible on the profile, and whether "Available now" / response rate shows anything. Then:

## 2. Landing page (done in `docs/index.html`, you push)

What changed and why:

- Default offer is now `?o=shots` (RSVP still at `?o=rsvp`).
- Two packages: **Starter ₹999** (5 products, 10 images) and **Catalogue ₹1,999** (12 products, 24 images, same model and light throughout). ₹1,999 is the anchor that makes free tools irrelevant; ₹999 is the entry.
- **Primary CTA is "Get my free preview"**: name, WhatsApp, shop handle, then a pre-filled WhatsApp message to you. "Pay by UPI now" is the secondary button for people already convinced.
- "Within the hour" is gone. It says **same day, 3 orders a day max**. You have a job.
- FAQ now answers the free-Gemini objection honestly ("for one photo use the free app; a catalogue is where it breaks").
- New funnel events: `package`, `preview_request`, `preview_whatsapp` (plus the old `visit`, `reserve`, `upi_intent`, `paid`).
- Tested headless: package switch, preview path, direct pay path, UTR confirmation, RSVP path, mobile layout. QR code now guarded so a blocked CDN cannot break the pay page.

To ship: `git pull`, look at `docs/index.html`, `git push`. GitHub Pages redeploys in about a minute. Then open the page on your phone and tap "Pay by UPI now" through to GPay to confirm the ₹ and name prefill (the brief's open to-do). Do not pay yourself.

Updated SQL for reading the funnel:

```sql
select offer, event, count(distinct lead_id) leads
from events where created_at > now() - interval '7 days'
group by 1,2
order by 1, array_position(array['visit','package','preview_request','preview_whatsapp','reserve','upi_intent','paid'], event);
```

## 3. Demo sets and orders in the Gemini app (Day 1, 1.5 hours, ₹0)

The app gives about 20 free images a day. Enough for two demo sets across two days and the first previews. Save every good image at full size.

**Demo set A (for Reel B1 and the gig gallery): a kurti or co-ord set, 6 to 8 shots.** Use one of your own family's or a friend's product, or buy nothing and use a plain garment photographed on a bed in daylight.

Prompt pattern (paste the product photo, then):

> Use this exact garment. Keep the print, colour, border and fabric identical to the photo, do not invent any pattern. Show it worn by a 26-year-old Indian woman, medium skin tone, shoulder-length dark hair, natural expression, standing in a sunlit cafe in Mumbai, shot on a 50mm lens, soft daylight, editorial fashion photo, full body, 4:5.

Then for consistency across the set, reply in the same chat:

> Same woman, same face, same hair, same light. Now standing on a quiet Bandra street in the evening. Full body, 4:5.

Repeat with: wedding guest at a daytime function; office lobby; plain grey studio. Keep every image in one chat so the model face stays the same. If the face drifts, say "same face as image 1" and regenerate.

**Demo set B (for Reel B2, the comparison Reel):** one deliberately bad free-tool style image where the print drifts (ask for the garment "in a similar style"), next to your checked one. That is the "cheap AI tools drift" frame.

**Preview for a real lead:** same first prompt, one image, then add the watermark. Free watermark: open the image in Canva (free) or your phone's markup, write `PREVIEW · @yourshop` diagonally at 40% opacity. Two minutes.

**Paid order:** same chat per customer, 2 to 3 attempts per product, pick the best, check print and hands, deliver 4:5 and 9:16 (crop in Canva). Budget your time at 10 minutes per product. A Starter order is under an hour. If the free daily limit blocks you mid-order, that is the moment to pay for the API (about ₹6 an image, so under ₹100 per order), because by then the customer has paid.

## 5. Five boutique DMs (Day 2, 30 min, ₹0)

This is the highest-leverage block and the one place I argue against the "no outreach" rule. Five messages, not a hundred. One yes gives you a targeted audience and your first real screenshot.

Who: Instagram boutiques with 2K to 15K followers, posting kurtis, co-ords, sarees or jewellery, product photos mostly flat-lays or mannequins, active in the last week, based in India. Find them through the hashtags above and through the "suggested" row under any boutique profile. Prefer Nagpur, Pune, Mumbai, Surat, Jaipur accounts (the owner can meet you later if it turns into something).

Message (from Avani's account, honest about who is behind it):

> Hi! I follow your kurtis, the prints are lovely. Quick offer, no catch: my friend Ayush (full-stack developer) built a tool that puts your products on an AI model, same print, same model across your whole set. We are testing it this week with 5 boutiques. Send me 1 product photo and you get 3 finished model shots free, same day. If you like them and post them, tag us, that is all we ask. If not, no hard feelings. Want to try?

If yes: deliver 3 shots the same day, ask "happy for me to screenshot your reply?", and offer the Starter at ₹999 for the rest of her products only after she has the free ones.

If no reply in 48 hours, do not follow up. Move on.

## 8. Kill rules (read on day 5, not before)

- **Zero preview requests** across 3 Reels, 5 DMs and the Fiverr gig: no demand at this reach. Stop. ₹1,000 untouched. Write it up.
- **Previews requested, nobody paid:** price or quality. Look at which one from the WhatsApp replies. Offer ₹499 for 3 products once. If still nothing, stop.
- **One stranger paid ₹999 or more** (Instagram or Fiverr): keep going to day 14. Now, and only now, section 9.
- A follower paying ₹299 for selfies, a friend paying, or a DM boutique posting the free shots is proof for screenshots, not a trigger for spending.

## 9. Money rules (the only thing that keeps the ₹1,000 safe)

- ₹0 until a stranger pays. Demo sets and previews in the Gemini app, not the API.
- After a stranger pays, the most you spend is **₹500** on one Meta ad, **lifetime budget** on the campaign (never a daily boost), on a **prepaid ad account**, on the Reel that produced the payment, targeting women 22 to 45 in tier-1/2 cities with boutique / saree / ethnic wear / small business interests. The other ₹500 stays untouched this week.
- Gemini API only for paid orders, and only when the free app limit blocks a delivery. About ₹6 an image. A Starter order costs under ₹100 to make.
- Worst case with these rules: ₹0 lost, 8.5 hours spent, a live page, two Fiverr gigs and three Reels on the account.
